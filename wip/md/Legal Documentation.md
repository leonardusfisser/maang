   markdown# Data Retention Policy

## Active White Labels

| Data Type | Retention | Reason |
|-----------|-----------|--------|
| Account info | Duration of account | Service provision |
| Content (media, posts) | Duration of account | Service provision |
| Login logs | 90 days | Security |
| Application logs | 90 days | Debugging |
| Payment records | 7 years after deletion | Legal requirement (Estonia/EU) |

## Deleted/Cancelled Accounts

| Data Type | Retention After Deletion | Reason |
|-----------|--------------------------|--------|
| Personal data | 30 days | Grace period for accidental deletion |
| Financial records | 7 years | Tax/legal compliance |
| Anonymized analytics | Indefinite | Cannot re-identify |

## Backups

- Automated backups every 6 hours
- Backups retained 90 days
- Deleted account data purged from backups after 90 days

## Legal Holds

If data subject to legal investigation/litigation:
- Retention period extended until matter resolved
- User notified if legally permissible
